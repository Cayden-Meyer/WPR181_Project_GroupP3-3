Our Services

Residential home cleaning

Coming home to a clean space shouldn't be a luxury. Our residential cleaning service covers every room in your home, top to bottom, using a consistent checklist so nothing gets missed. Whether it's a once-off deep clean or a regular weekly or bi-weekly visit. Our cleaners bring their own supplies and equipment, so all you need to do is unlock the door

A standard Clean includes:

  • Dusting of all surfaces, shelves, skirting boards, and light fixtures
  • Vacuuming and mopping of all floors
  • Full bathroom clean: toilets, showers, tubs, sinks, and mirrors
  • Kitchen surfaces, stovetop, sink, and exterior of appliances wiped down
  • Cleaning of interior windows and glass doors within reach
  • Interior glass and mirror cleaning

For an additional fee, we can also take care of deeper work like leather conditioning, carpet shampooing, headlight restoration, or engine bay cleaning. Just let us know what you need when you book, and we'll add it to your quote.

Car detailing service

Your car works hard for you every day, it deserves more than a quick rinse at the gas station. Our detailing service goes deep, inside and out, to bring back that just-off-the-lot feeling. Whether it's a quick refresh or a full restoration job, our detailers bring their own equipment and products, so all you need to do is hand over the keys.

A standard detail includes:

  • Hand wash, dry, and exterior wax
  • Wheel and tire cleaning, including brake dust removal
  • Full interior vacuum, including trunk and under seats
  • Dashboard, console, and door panel wipe-down

A clean workplace isn't just about appearances, it keeps your team healthy, focused, and proud of where they work. Our crews bring their own supplies and equipment, so there's no disruption to your operations.

A standard Clean includes:

  • Dusting of desks, shelves, and shared surfaces
  • Vacuuming and mopping of all floors
  • Full restroom clean and restocking of supplies
  • Kitchenette and breakroom surfaces wiped down
  • Emptying and relining of all trash bins

For an additional fee, we can also take care of tasks like interior window washing, carpet shampooing, or deep sanitizing of high-touch surfaces. Just let us know what you need when you book, and we'll add it to your quote.

Our kitchen deep cleaning service tackles the grease and buildup that daily wipe-downs can't reach, getting your kitchen inspection-ready and running safely. Our team brings commercial-grade equipment and works around your service hours to avoid downtime.

Move-Out deep clean

Getting your deposit back shouldn't come down to how many hours you spend scrubbing on moving day. Our move-out cleaning service covers everything landlords and property managers check for, so you can hand back the keys with confidence. Whether you're moving out of an apartment or a house, our cleaners bring their own supplies and follow a full end-of-lease checklist.

For an additional fee, we can also take care of tasks like inside-oven and inside-fridge cleaning, carpet shampooing, or wall spot cleaning. Just let us know what you need when you book, and we'll add it to your quote.
